# Wildlings credits

**Creature art:** made by the Tuxemon community (https://www.tuxemon.org,
https://github.com/Tuxemon/Tuxemon). Every creature's artists and licence are
listed in the app (Wildlings → Dex → Credits) and in Tuxemon's ATTRIBUTIONS.md.
Most are CC BY-SA 4.0. Some are CC BY 4.0 or CC BY-SA 3.0.

**Changes made for this app:** the sprites are recoloured to match their
Wildlings type and turned into 16-frame idle animations (squash, stretch and
sway). The changed sprites are shared under the same licence as the originals.
The scripts that make these changes are in `tools/wildlings-sprites.py` and
`tools/wildlings-pick.py`.

**Provinces, gym sites and Elite Four sites:** Natural Earth (public domain).
**Town names:** GeoNames (CC BY 4.0). See `tools/provinces-build.py`.
