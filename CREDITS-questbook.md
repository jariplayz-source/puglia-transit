# Questbook art credits

The painted scenes and portraits in Questbook (`app/assets/rq/`) come from **Battle for Wesnoth** (https://www.wesnoth.org, https://github.com/wesnoth/wesnoth), made by the Wesnoth artists. They are licensed under the **GNU General Public License, version 2 or later**. The images were resized and converted to WebP; nothing else was changed.

The Questbook stories, rules and code are original to this app. The game's source parts are in `tools/questbook/` (joined into `app/assets/rpg.js`).
