      🆘 Emergency & practical

      • 112: general emergency number (police, ambulance, fire), free from any phone.
      • 118: medical emergency / ambulance.
      • Pharmacy: look for the green cross. A sign on every pharmacy door lists the farmacia di turno, the one open at night and on Sundays.
      • Taxis, Brindisi: ranks at the train station (Piazza Crispi) and the airport. Radio Taxi is the airport's official taxi company. Airport → centre ≈ €15–20.
      • Taxis, Lecce: Radio Taxi Lecce H24: +39 320 832 1779. Ranks at the station and Porta Napoli.
      • Long taxi rides are expensive (e.g. Brindisi airport → Lecce ≈ €65). Agree the price before you set off.
      • Lunch break: many shops, churches and small museums close roughly 12:30/13:00–16:00/17:00.

      📍 Brindisi basics

      • Brindisi Centrale (train station) is at Piazza Crispi, at the top of Corso Umberto. It's about 15 min on foot to the harbour.
      • The airport bus (STP “AP”) stops right outside the station.
      • FSE buses to Manduria leave from the bus stops near the station. Check the stop in the app's “Autobus FSE” details.

      🎟️ Tickets

      • Trenitalia app sells both Trenitalia and FSE (Ferrovie del Sud Est) trains and buses, including combined journeys with a change in Lecce. A digital ticket needs no stamping.
      • Paper regional tickets: stamp (validate) them in the machine on the platform before boarding, or you can be fined.
      • Regional tickets have a fixed price and no seat reservations, so buying on the day is fine.
      • Frecciarossa (fast trains, e.g. to Bari) have reserved seats and get cheaper when booked early.
      • At Bari Centrale, the Matera (FAL) and Bari-airport (Ferrotramviaria) trains leave from separate stations beside the main one.
